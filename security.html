Data sovereignty

Two places it can live

You pick where the system sits before we build anything, and that choice changes none of the controls further down.

Single-tenant VPC in the EU

We operate and monitor the system in a dedicated European VPC. Nothing is shared with another client, and data never leaves the region you choose.

Operated by Farvex

On-premise, on your servers

The entire stack runs on your own internal servers, air-gapped if your policy demands it. You hold the keys, the logs and the hardware.

Operated by you
  • No data resale — we do not monetise your data and we pull no telemetry out of it.
  • On-premise ready — every component can run entirely on your own internal servers.
  • 24/7 security operations — automated vulnerability scanning runs against the deployment without pause.
  • Risk-classified under the EU AI Act — every system we put into production is classified and fully auditable.
Controls

Six controls that hold on every deployment

The same rules apply whether we run the system in a European VPC or you run it on your own servers.

No training on your data

Your documents, OCR text and ERP entries are never used to train or calibrate third-party foundation models. That guarantee is legally binding.

Legally binding

EU data residency

Every compute instance sits inside a European data centre — the Netherlands, Germany or France — under EU data custody law. GDPR and the Dutch AVG apply throughout.

EU only

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Traffic is protected by TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256, and you hold the keys in an HSM.

AES-256 and TLS 1.3

Single-tenant infrastructure

Enterprise deployments run on physically isolated single-tenant infrastructure, with dedicated compute nodes and private IP ranges.

Dedicated tenancy

Append-only audit logs

Immutable cryptographic append-only trails record every inference request, access event and ERP sync.

Tamper-proof

Deterministic circuit breakers

Agents work inside strict deterministic bounds. Any anomaly or out-of-bounds parameter trips an immediate safe freeze.

Safe freeze
Compliance

The frameworks we build against

Farvex is built to satisfy the requirements below. The status column states precisely where each one stands.

Compliance frameworks, what each covers and its current status at Farvex
Framework What it covers Status
GDPR / Dutch AVG European and Dutch rules for handling personal data. In place
EU AI Act Risk classification and auditability of deployed AI systems. Risk-classified
ISO 27001 The standard for managing information security. Built to meet
SOC 2 Type II An audit of how security controls perform over a period of time. Built to meet
DNB (Dutch Central Bank) Supervision of Dutch financial institutions and their suppliers. Built to meet
AFM The Dutch financial markets authority. Built to meet

“Built to meet” means the controls are designed against that framework. It is not a claim of certification. We hand over our control descriptions and evidence whenever your auditors ask for them.
