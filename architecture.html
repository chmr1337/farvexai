The route

The route every document takes

Each job passes through the same five layers in the same order. Nothing is skipped, and every layer records what it did.

  1. 01

    A file lands

    A PDF, a scan, an email or an API call reaches the gateway. Its signature is checked and it is queued before anything reads it.

  2. 02

    The page is read

    Vision models lift fields and tables off the page, handwriting, stamps and customs codes included.

  3. 03

    Your rules decide

    Checksums, VAT numbers and IBANs are verified against your business rules before a single value is written anywhere.

  4. 04

    The record is written

    It lands in Exact Online, SAP or your database as one committed transaction — all of it, or none of it.

  5. 05

    The run is logged and watched

    Throughput, latency and model drift reach your dashboards, and every step is appended to a write-once audit log.

Layer 03

Business rules

Your rules run as a finite state machine: fixed transitions, no open-ended loops. When a check fails, a circuit breaker stops the job rather than letting the system guess.

Deployment

Three places it can run

Three options. The software is identical in all three; only the ground under it changes.

EU managed cloud

We host and operate it in an EU region you pick. Your data stays in that region and we carry the on-call rota.

Your own cloud account

The same stack deployed into your AWS, Azure or GCP tenant. You hold the keys, the network rules and the bill.

On-premise

Runs on your own hardware, fully disconnected if that is what you need. Models ship as signed containers you install yourself.

That choice decides where the machines sit, not how the system behaves. The audit log, the monitoring and the fallback rules are identical in all three.
