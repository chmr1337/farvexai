Logistics and freight · Rotterdam

Customs paperwork cleared and booked without anyone retyping it

EuroFreight Logistics B.V. moves 12,000 international shipping bills a month. Every one used to be typed in by hand, so invoices waited up to 48 hours in a queue and the team burned roughly €35,000 a month in overtime. Farvex now parses and validates each bill in 118 ms and writes it straight into Exact Online.

Before · typed by hand

  • Eight full-time staff spent two days clearing every batch of bills.
  • Invoices sat up to 48 hours before anyone opened them.
  • Roughly €35,000 a month in overtime, purely to keep pace with volume.
  • Mistyped VAT and IBAN fields blocked payments.

After · running on Farvex

  • Every bill is parsed and validated in 118 ms, day or night.
  • IBANs are checked against the Dutch bank checksum before anything is written.
  • Cleared bills arrive in Exact Online on their own, with a full audit trail.
  • Staff see only the exceptions the system could not settle.

Farvex rebuilt our back office. Work that took eight full-time staff two days to clear is now posted to Exact Online before the ship has finished docking.

Financial services · Amsterdam

VAT reconciliation and IBAN checks across 400-plus client firms

Nexus Corporate Finance reconciles European VAT for more than 400 mid-market companies. Agents match every transaction, run Rabobank IBAN checks and flag whatever will not reconcile, so reviewers only ever see exceptions. There have been no audit penalties since go-live, and the firm passed its Dutch Central Bank audit.

400+ Client firms covered
100% Data held in the EU
<140 ms Mean API response
0 Penalties since go-live
  1. 1

    Transactions land

    Ledger exports and bank statements arrive through the API from each client's accounting system.

  2. 2

    VAT rules run

    Every line is matched against the European VAT rates and reverse-charge rules that apply to that client.

  3. 3

    IBAN checked

    Counterparty IBANs are validated against the Rabobank check and the Dutch checksum before anything is approved.

  4. 4

    Exceptions raised

    Anything that will not reconcile goes to a reviewer with the reason attached. The rest posts and is logged for the audit trail.

The compliance guarantees, and the fact that nothing leaves the EU, are what carried us through the Dutch Central Bank audit. Enterprise grade without the trade-offs.

Marloes van der Meer

Chief Risk Officer, Nexus Corporate Finance

Three decisions both deployments made

Different sectors, identical choices where it counted.

It writes into the system you already use

Exact Online at EuroFreight, the client ledgers at Nexus. Nobody had to learn a new interface.

Nothing is written until it has been checked

IBAN checksums, VAT rules and totals are validated first. A record that fails goes to a person, not into the ledger.

The data never leaves the EU

Both run on EU infrastructure with no third-country transfer, which is what made the audits uneventful.
