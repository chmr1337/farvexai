What manual handling actually costs

The same workload under two operating models. The left column is where most finance and logistics teams sit today.

Handled by people

  • The same figures get keyed in three times — once from the email, once into the spreadsheet, once into the ERP.
  • Extra volume can only be absorbed by hiring.
  • Fatigue pushes keying errors past 8%, and late shipments follow.
  • Nobody can answer "what happened last month" without rebuilding it from inboxes.

Handled by a pipeline

  • Parsing and posting finish in roughly a tenth of a second.
  • Ten times the volume costs the same to compute.
